# Food 360

## Project Overview

Food 360 is a digital transparency platform designed to provide consumers with comprehensive insights into the food products they purchase. By utilizing a unique Digital Product Passport (DPP) for every item, the project aims to improve consumer safety and streamline the product exporting process.

## Features

  * **QR Code Integration**: Users can scan a QR code on food packaging to be instantly directed to the product's detailed information page.
  * **Digital Product Passport (DPP)**: Every product carries a unique digital identity that contains its full lifecycle and safety data.
  * **Ingredient & Sourcing Transparency**: Provides a complete list of ingredients, specifying whether they are locally sourced (e.g., wheat or sugar from India) or imported.
  * **Health & Safety Alerts**: Identifies specific preservatives used and lists potential medical side effects, such as migraines, skin irritation, or asthma symptoms.
  * **Dietary Status**: Clearly indicates the vegan or non-vegan status of the product.
  * **Manufacturer Information**: Displays the manufacturer's name and precise location.
  * **Usage Guides**: Includes a "How to Use" section featuring video demonstrations via YouTube thumbnails.

## Technical Demonstration

The current prototype demonstrates these features using **Britannia Brown Bread** as a case study:

  * **Product Identification**: Britannia Brown Bread, 400g, priced at 40 Rupees.
  * **Manufacturer**: Britannia Industries Limited, Bangalore, India.
  * **Preservative Analysis**:
      * **E282 (Calcium Propionate)**: Linked to potential migraines.
      * **E200 (Sorbic Acid)**: Linked to potential skin irritation and asthma.
  * **Sourcing**: Wheat, wheat flour, and sugar identified as being derived from India.

## Benefits

  * **Export Ready**: Enhances the appeal and compliance of products in the global market by providing a standardized Digital Product Passport.
  * **Informed Consumption**: Empowers users to make healthier choices based on detailed preservative and ingredient data.
